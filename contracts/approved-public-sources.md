# Approved Public Game Sources

Production qualification is a research-entrypoint permission, not unrestricted factual trust. The validated machine-readable index is `gold-assets/sources/registry.json`.

## Last War Official Wiki
`https://wiki.lastwar.com/en/` is approved for on-demand external research. Pages under `wiki.lastwar.com` have mixed authorship (game publisher announcements, official season information, credited player guides, stories, open comments). Classify the individual article, not the domain, before using its claims.

- Publisher/editorial feature notices, detailed rules, patch notes: official *published/announced* evidence for relevant dates and cohorts. Confirm current live applicability and preview versus release status.
- Credited player guides: community-tier evidence; verify independently or against current direct in-game observations before consequential advice.
- Player replies, comments and stories: never elevate to mechanic-source authority.
- Image-only tables/maps: if relevant detail cannot be read, say unverified instead of fabricating numbers.
- Record article URL, published/updated date, affected server/season and any in-game discrepancy disclaimer.
- Current verified in-game text and authoritative private canonical state take precedence. Official-hosted articles never authorize spending without the actual current transaction screen and balances.
- Do not mass import claims automatically or republish article bodies into shared Gold Assets. Only vetted, sanitized, source-attributed facts may enter a future knowledge pack through ordinary change control.
