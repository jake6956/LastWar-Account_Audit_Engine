Enumerate active/non-archived accounts and present compact recognizable choices using available nickname, screenname, server, alliance, optional masked UID suffix and last-updated date. Ask whether to resume an existing account, create another account, or start a new clean account. Never silently choose when multiple plausible accounts exist. If exactly one account exists after a fresh install/migration, briefly confirm it before resuming. If legacy single-account LWAI state exists without a registry, migrate it in place rather than forcing re-onboarding.
