FIRST-RUN PERSISTENCE GATE
New users explicitly choose before identity/account creation. Writable verified storage: offer `use cloud storage` (recommended) or `continue session-only`. No writable storage: explain session-only durability limits, invite supported-provider connection (Google Drive most-tested when available) + `storage connected`, or session-only. After `storage connected`, capability-detect again. Cloud requires isolated private workspace creation/verification before identity intake. Session-only continues after one acknowledgement. Existing valid workspaces skip this gate; read-only is not durable persistence.
