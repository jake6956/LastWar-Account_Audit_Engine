RESEARCH
Do not optimize by tree completion percentage. Model prerequisites and cumulative cost/time to target breakpoints. Treat each Tech Center as an independent scheduling lane. Compare mandatory season progression, universal combat, primary squad, counter/specialist development, PvP systems and prerequisite overhead. Avoid low-value healing/load/resource nodes unless strategically justified or required.

MULTI-QUEUE SCHEDULING
Optimize queues together. Track current research, target path, ETA if known, strategic lane and blockers. Prefer parallel lanes that avoid duplicative bottlenecks. Before a major path recommendation, calculate best-available total prerequisites/cost/time and state what would invalidate it.

SQUAD-SLOT TECH
Track the actual squad slot receiving each formation. Do not confuse squad number with strength rank. Preserve completed/max slot-tech states as canonical facts.

DRONE / COMPONENTS / CHIPS
Track Drone level, Combat Boost milestones, attributes, components, chip presets/specialization, stars and deterministic choice resources. Prefer nearby meaningful breakpoints. If chip progression is RNG, do not fabricate an exact sequence. Make precise chip-choice recommendations only when deterministic selection resources exist.

DECORATIONS
Treat Decorations as permanent combat progression. Track universal/unit-specific Attack/Defense/HP, crit/skill/damage effects and nonlinear breakpoints. Compare material/currency use against other permanent progression.

PROFESSION / GLOBAL BONUSES
Audit combat-relevant Profession skills and include Alliance bonuses, march size/morale and other global systems when they can explain real performance differences.

SEASON PROGRESSION SYSTEMS
Treat each major season subsystem as its own resource lane with current target, next breakpoint, remaining cost and confidence. Do not assume costs/mechanics transfer unchanged between seasons.
