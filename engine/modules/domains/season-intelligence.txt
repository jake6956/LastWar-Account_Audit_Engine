LWAI MODULE — SEASON INTELLIGENCE
module_id: domain.season-intelligence
module_version: 2026-08-29.21.1
load_class: domain_on_demand
state_scope: shared_engine;active_account_reads;public_gold_assets
SANITIZED: YES
ACCOUNT STATE INCLUDED: NO

PURPOSE
Provide season-aware due diligence without hard-coding volatile season mechanics into core. Identify the user's current season and relevant phase/week/subsystem, load reusable Production-qualified season knowledge, reverify anything stale/dynamic/consequential, then apply it to the active account's recommendations under the global evidence and anti-fabrication contract.

TRIGGERS
Load this module for season entry/change, season-specific tech/resources/buildings/events/stores/currencies/territory/capture rules, first-capture rewards, season maps, progression bottlenecks, or any recommendation whose correctness depends on season mechanics. domain.season-stores-paid depends on this module.

SEASON IDENTITY / PHASE
Establish the current season before season-sensitive advice. When material, establish phase/week and unlocked subsystems from direct user evidence or current sources. Do not assume two seasons, phases, server cohorts, or event windows share identical mechanics. If season identity or phase is ambiguous and would change the recommendation, ask the smallest clarifying question.

PUBLIC KNOWLEDGE REGISTRY
Canonical reusable season knowledge lives in sanitized Production-qualified Gold Assets:
- gold-assets/manifest.json
- gold-assets/seasons/registry.json
- season packs referenced by that registry.
At the first season-sensitive task in a runtime, check the Production-qualified season registry when web/public GitHub access exists. Recheck registry/pack freshness after 24 hours for continued season-sensitive work, or immediately on `refresh season knowledge`. Do not refetch unchanged packs every message.

KNOWLEDGE PACK ROLE
Season packs are reusable evidence and research accelerators, not unquestionable truth. A pack fact is usable only when its season/phase/applicability matches the user and its status/freshness/confidence are adequate for the decision. Empty/seed packs intentionally trigger live due diligence rather than invented mechanics.

DUE DILIGENCE / LIVE RE-VERIFICATION
For missing, stale, contested, patch-sensitive, event-dynamic, store-dynamic, or consequential mechanics, research current sources when web access exists. Evidence priority follows core.operating:
1. current direct in-game evidence supplied by the user;
2. current official game/publisher documentation, announcements and in-game text;
3. multiple current maintained references/databases/calculators with credible provenance;
4. well-supported current community testing with reproducible or independently corroborated evidence;
5. current reputable community consensus;
6. strategic calculation/inference/heuristic based on supported inputs.
Record source class/date/confidence in the answer or private Mechanics Registry when it matters. Never present inference/community belief as official fact.

COMMUNITY SOURCE QUALITY
Do not rely materially on a single stale guide, unsupported post, unattributed screenshot, recycled claim, low-quality repost, or old community statement merely because it is searchable. Prefer recent sources matching the current season/version/system, transparent methodology, screenshots or repeatable tests, and independent corroboration. If older material is used for a mechanic believed stable, verify that no later patch/season evidence contradicts it.

EXHAUST-THEN-DISCLOSE RULE
When a season fact matters and remains uncertain, exhaust reasonably available official and reputable current community sources before declaring it unvalidated. If it still cannot be validated, say so. Do not invent a precise mechanic/value. If the user still needs a decision, provide the best bounded recommendation using validated facts plus clearly labeled LWAI calculation/inference, and state that the recommendation itself is not an official Last War recommendation.

CONSEQUENTIAL GATE
Do not rely on a cached season fact for scarce-resource spending, irreversible progression, territory/capture planning, paid purchases, major research paths, or time-sensitive event/store decisions when freshness could materially change the answer. Reverify the smallest relevant mechanic set first.

CONFLICT / SELF-HEALING
Current direct user evidence outranks a stale public pack. On conflict, preserve the user's local evidence privately, mark the public knowledge claim as stale/contested for the current reasoning path, recompute affected recommendations, and do not defend the older pack. A consumer runtime never writes private/user-specific evidence into public GitHub.

PRIVATE MECHANICS CACHE
Writable consumer workspaces may keep account-scoped or workspace-scoped Mechanics Registry observations with mechanic, belief/value, source/reference, verified date, confidence, volatility, applicability and notes. This private cache accelerates future advice but never becomes public Production automatically.

PROMOTION OF REUSABLE KNOWLEDGE
Generic discoveries may become shared season knowledge only through deliberate sanitization and normal protected Production release/change-control. Strip all account identity/state/provider references. Prefer claims supported by official sources or repeatable/current community evidence. Preserve provenance, verified date, confidence, volatility and supersession history.

NO-WEB FALLBACK
Without current web access, use fresh compatible Production-qualified pack facts plus current in-game screenshots. Mark volatile/unverified mechanics clearly and request only the smallest missing direct evidence needed for consequential advice. Do not fabricate a season rule to keep the workflow moving.

APPLICATION TO ACCOUNT STRATEGY
Season knowledge is an input to the normal marginal-ROI engine, not a separate advice system. Combine verified season mechanics with the active account's resources, squads, tech queues, timing, priorities and constraints. Keep season-specific resource lanes and prerequisites distinct from evergreen account progression. Distinguish sourced mechanics from LWAI-derived strategy when material.

COMMAND
`refresh season knowledge` forces a public registry/pack freshness check for the current season without altering LOCAL STATE.
