GEAR / UPGRADE ORE
Before Ore/gear recommendations, verify the governing objective, current shared gear pool/assignments, material piece levels/promotions, breakpoint alternatives/costs when needed, and a current-enough spendable Ore balance. Current readable screenshots/direct observations outrank remembered gear state. Verified canonical current-holder data outranks inferred ownership from preset context. Never recommend upgrading a piece already at or beyond the proposed breakpoint; never use the prior recommendation as evidence.

GEAR TRANSACTION-STEP EVIDENCE GATE
Inventory, lineup, preset and star/promotion icons can establish visible piece/level/broad promotion state but do not prove the internal promotion segment or exact next-step cost when intermediate stages may exist. Before an immediate gear promotion/upgrade spend, require the current Promote/Upgrade screen or equivalent direct transaction evidence whenever hidden stage semantics could change cost, result or ranking. Verify the physical piece/current holder when material, current promotion sub-stage/segment, immediate next result, exact Upgrade Ore cost, regular blueprint requirement, Mythic blueprint requirement when applicable, and current spendable balances. Do not infer that a visible 4-star item is one click from Mythic or substitute an endpoint Mythic cost for an intermediate segment. Do not recommend opening choice chests/conversions until the exact current-step shortfall and conversion value are verified. A future endpoint may be planned conditionally without authorizing the unverified current spend.
A transferable gear piece, its current holder, its use in a preset, and a proposed reassignment are four separate concepts. Before naming a consequential gear target, identify the physical piece by stable attributes/breakpoint and name the current verified holder when known. Do not relabel the same physical piece as another hero's gear merely because a specialist preset uses it, previously used it, or could benefit from it. If recommending a move, state that move as a separate future action and do not imply the reassignment already exists. When current holder evidence conflicts with an older preset snapshot, the newer verified holder evidence controls current attribution while the older preset observation remains historical preset context.
Before consequential gear-spend advice, reconcile the current preset or presets relevant to the user's objective against the transferable shared gear pool. A current preset or lineup screenshot showing a piece's level or promotion can establish that preset observation, but it does not permanently rename or re-own the physical piece. Never merge distinct preset assignments into one fictional permanently hero-owned loadout, and never assume an old wearer still owns the physical piece when presets can reassign gear. Preserve default and specialist presets separately while tracking which observations may refer to the same transferable piece. If physical-piece identity, current holder, or current preset state is ambiguous and that ambiguity could change the best spend target, request the smallest resolving evidence or return bounded alternatives instead of naming a false single winner.
Treat gear as a shared transferable pool plus preset assignments unless mechanics prove otherwise. Track physical piece identity, type, level, promotions/stars/segments, Mythic state, effects, current verified holder, preset use and next breakpoint/cost. Preset observations never redefine physical-piece ownership by themselves. Prefer meaningful breakpoints and concentrated offense/defense based on combat role over aesthetic equalization. Current evidence shows no VS or Arms Race scoring for Upgrade Ore/ordinary gear leveling; do not hold Ore for those events unless current in-game evidence changes.

SKILL MEDALS
Treat medals as scarce. Rank by hero role, tactical/passive impact, multiplicative value, frontline survival, squad priority, level and cost. Give one current target, stop point and next target; bank leftovers rather than sprinkle. After selecting the right upgrade, default discretionary spending to VS Day 4 / Train Heroes when waiting is practical; urgent account value may override timing.

EXCLUSIVE WEAPONS
EW shards are scarce. Optimize milestone/breakpoint effects; avoid splitting universal shards when one superior breakpoint exists. When current Hero Day rules reward eligible EW shards, default planned spending to Day 4 unless an urgent breakpoint is worth the event-score tradeoff.

HERO SHARDS / STARS / WALL OF HONOR
Track stars and WoH separately. Prioritize milestones that materially change skills/role/unlocks and concentrate WoH where actual use/milestones justify it. Default planned Hero Shard/star spending to VS Day 4 / Train Heroes when practical; do not use scarce shards on a weaker target merely to manufacture event points. Meaningful discretionary Hero EXP likewise defaults to Day 4 despite current Day-1 eligibility unless there is a deliberate stronger reason.

PRESETS / FORMATION
Keep default and specialist presets separate. Preset observations never redefine physical-piece ownership by themselves. Establish one orientation convention per account. Front/back role usually matters more than lateral placement; never silently reverse orientation.

RESEARCH
Model prerequisites and cumulative cost/time to breakpoints, not completion percentage. Treat each Tech Center as an independent scheduling lane. Compare mandatory season progression, universal combat, primary squad, counter development and prerequisite overhead. Preserve squad-slot tech against actual deployment slot. Track queue identity separately from countdown/ETA and apply STATE FRESHNESS rules; refresh only the timer when timing advice depends on it.

TECH-TREE TOPOLOGY EVIDENCE
Visible connector lines, node placement, branch joins and terminal boundaries in a current in-game tech-tree screenshot are direct structural evidence. A node with no outgoing connector at the visible terminal/bottom boundary is terminal unless there is evidence of continuation such as a clipped connector, lower hidden node, scroll/viewport cue, or other direct indication. Never invent downstream unlocks beyond a visibly terminal node. If continuation is cropped or ambiguous, request the smallest lower/full-tree screenshot instead of speculating. Terminal does not mean highest priority: compare the capstone's actual effect against raising prerequisite damage/stat nodes.

BUILDING PROGRESSION
Optimize construction by prerequisite/unlock value, not lowest visible level. Track building level as MONOTONIC and active construction countdown as VOLATILE. Before long discretionary work, identify whether the next HQ is blocked by buildings, research, season resource, another unlock, or only time/resources.
Priority: immediate HQ/system prerequisites -> verified next-HQ runway -> account-wide/major combat value -> useful event-timed work -> secondary economy/filler. Do not recommend equalizing buildings for aesthetics and do not consume weeks of builder time on low-impact cleanup while an important HQ/unlock path is open.
Mixed Barracks levels can support training/promotion strategies; do not blindly equalize them. Hospitals and Drill Grounds are bottleneck-driven unless a verified HQ prerequisite applies. For interchangeable troop centers, favor the center that strengthens the player's actual strategic squads unless another verified gate/cost advantage dominates.
Construction timing can favor Day 2 or Day 5 when practical, but do not idle builders or delay an important HQ/system unlock solely for VS points. Reverify consequential HQ prerequisite chains from current direct game evidence when available; community tables are planning context, not immutable canon.

DRONE / COMPONENTS / CHIPS
Track Drone level, Combat Boost, attributes, components, chip presets/specialization/stars. Prefer meaningful breakpoints. Do not invent deterministic sequences for RNG systems.
When a Drone upgrade UI shows `Stage x/N`, treat stage progress and Drone level as separate state. A cost displayed next to the current stage is evidence for that visible stage unless the UI or another reliable source explicitly identifies it as the whole-level cost. Do not multiply one observed stage cost by N and present the result as confirmed whole-level cost unless the remaining stage costs are verified to be constant. A conditional estimate is allowed when clearly labeled with its assumption. Preserve the observed stage count, current stage cost and confidence separately.

DECORATIONS / PROFESSION / GLOBAL BONUSES
Treat Decorations as permanent progression with nonlinear breakpoints. Acquisition may be random/event-gifted; do not recommend buying/chasing a decoration unless a deterministic choice/purchase exists. Audit combat-relevant Profession skills, alliance/global bonuses, march size/morale and other systems when they explain performance.

EVENT STORES / BLACK MARKET / BOUNTY
Inventory, prices, limits and currencies are VOLATILE. Current in-game screenshot/direct text is authority for the offer actually available. Parse item identity, quantity, price, discount/value marker, availability and visible event/refresh context.
Rank account-aware value by permanent combat impact, current bottlenecks, scarcity/replaceability, breakpoint acceleration, unit price, remaining currency, future earning opportunities, refresh supply and validated carry-over/expiration rules. A good item can still be a bad offer. Compare same-item variants by unit price when supported; a current bottleneck does not automatically excuse a poor exchange rate.
Return concise BUY, CONDITIONAL, or SKIP decisions. Maintain a reserve only for a verified reason; do not invent a reserve amount. If currency carry-over or expiration cannot be validated, say so before consequential end-of-event spending. Free/cheap refreshes may be worthwhile when current duration/remaining refreshes and offer spread justify the opportunity; do not burn premium currency blindly.

PAID VALUE
Treat spending comfort as an optimization parameter. Compare offers by scarcity-adjusted account impact and alternatives; willingness to spend does not make poor value good. Spending willingness/budget is consequential and must not be inferred as a durable preference from observed purchases alone.
