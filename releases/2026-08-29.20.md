# 2026-08-29.20 — Season Intelligence Framework

Engine API: 1.0  
Workspace schema: 2.3  
Migration: engine-only; preserve LOCAL STATE; no re-onboarding or account rewrite.

## Added

- New on-demand `domain.season-intelligence` module for season identity/phase resolution, due diligence, current-source re-verification and self-healing against stronger direct evidence.
- Production-qualified `gold-assets/seasons/registry.json` plus a versioned `season-knowledge/1` pack schema.
- Seed Season 1, Season 2 and Season 3 knowledge packs. Seed packs intentionally contain no unverified mechanic claims and cause live due diligence when knowledge is missing.
- Public knowledge freshness: check on the first season-sensitive task per runtime; recheck after 24 hours of continued season-sensitive work; `refresh season knowledge` forces an immediate check.
- Consequential season gate for scarce resources, irreversible progression, territory/capture planning, major research, paid purchases and time-sensitive store/event decisions.
- Private Mechanics Registry behavior for user-specific observations; those observations never become public Gold Assets automatically.
- CI regressions enforcing module reachability, dependency wiring, sanitized packs, Gold Asset registration, standalone-fallback parity and loader-size discipline.

## Integration

`domain.season-stores-paid` now depends on `domain.season-intelligence`, so season/store questions automatically bring the knowledge layer into the active module graph. Verified season mechanics feed the normal marginal-ROI/account strategy engine rather than creating a separate recommendation system.

## Privacy

Public season knowledge is generic and sanitized. No UID, screenname, alliance/server identity, screenshots, balances, account database state, Corrections, battle history, provider IDs/paths or credentials are permitted in shared packs.
